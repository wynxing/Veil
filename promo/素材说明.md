## 宿舍插画

文件：`public/dorm.png`。

2026-10-04 使用内置 imagegen 生成，非 CLI/API 回退。没有参考既有角色或游戏画面。使用成年原创人物、原创恋爱游戏角色、温馨宿舍情境。

最终生成提示词：

```text

Asset type: master wide illustration for a playful Chinese software promo motion comic, landscape 16:9, ideally 2560x1440 or greater.
Primary request: Original cozy Chinese college dorm at night, a funny visual privacy moment. Right half: a young ADULT college student with short dark slightly messy hair, oversized mint sweatshirt, sitting tucked comfortably on lower bunk bed against lavender pillows, delighted and slightly blushing while playing an anime romance game on a handheld TABLET. Left half: their desk and LARGE COMPUTER MONITOR facing the viewer, conspicuously showing the SAME original pink-haired ADULT anime heroine as the tablet, with glowing pink screen. A second young ADULT roommate sits at the desk on the far left, seen from behind/three-quarter back, wearing navy headphones and casually beginning to glance over; no mocking expression. Both devices show a matching closeup of the heroine smiling in a wholesome pastel visual novel scene, no text. Tablet display must be visible to viewer, plausible hand pose.
Style/medium: stylish contemporary editorial manga illustration, clean dark navy ink outlines with variable thickness, flat pastel cel shading, subtle paper grain, expressive but polished characters, friendly visual humor, appealing storybook animation art. Not photoreal, not childish clipart, not a UI mockup.
Composition/framing: one contiguous horizontal room. The left monitor centered around x=28%, y=52%; right protagonist and tablet around x=76%, y=55%. Camera can crop to right half for first shot, then pull out to expose left monitor. No physical overlap between the two subjects. Reserve top 12% and bottom 15% for later overlays; no baked in typography, no borders, no split panels.
Environment: small tidy dorm with bunk bed at right, desk at left, soft desk lamp, trailing plant, a few books, tiny moon out window. Keep room detail subordinate to devices. No brand logos or identifiable copyrighted characters.
Lighting/mood: cozy warm amber desk light, dusky lavender blue room, coral-pink device glow, cheerful rather than voyeuristic.
Color palette: warm cream, lavender, mint, coral pink, navy outlines.
Constraints: all characters are adults. No nudity or sexualized imagery. No words, letters, captions, speech bubbles, text, logos or watermarks anywhere. Devices powered on in this asset. Make the pink-haired game heroine clearly the same on both screens.
```

插画以近景裁切和镜头拉远用于开头样片。没有对生成的位图进行后期图像编辑，文字和漫画图形为独立动画叠加层。
